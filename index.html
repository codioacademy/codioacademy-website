About Codio Academy

We don't teach lessons.
We build builders.

Codio Academy started with one belief: every learner has the raw material to become a creator — they just need the right studio, the right tools, and mentors who take their ideas seriously.

We blend technology, creativity and entrepreneurship into one hands-on curriculum where students don't just learn how things work — they build things that work. A robot. A playable game. A working app. A pitch deck. Real output, every term.

Mentors, not lecturersSmall groups guided by builders who ship real projects themselves.
Made for every ageCurriculum that grows in depth from age 6 all the way to 25+.
Outcomes you can seeEvery track ends in a portfolio piece, not just a certificate.

Mission

To equip the next generation with the technical, creative and entrepreneurial skills to turn their ideas into real, working projects — and the confidence to keep building beyond the classroom.

Vision

A generation of young creators, founders and innovators across the region who learned to build first, and ask permission second.

Why families choose us

Project-based terms, age-matched tracks, transparent progress updates for parents, and a studio environment built specifically for young creators — not a scaled-down adult classroom.
